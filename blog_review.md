The research paper Can Your Camera Tell If You’re Bored in Class? explains how computer vision can be used to identify the facial expression of a person to understand the level of attention.This paper describes the use of MediaPipe Face Mesh, which detects different points on a person’s face.The points can then be used to detect the movements in the eyes , mouth , eyebrows which is then used to identify whether a student is interested, tired or bored.

What I found most interesting was how something that normally feels very subjective, like saying “this student looks bored,” can be converted into data using facial landmarks.I had thought that Computer Vision can be used for objects or facial recognition so this gave me a new perspective of how it can be appiled to human behaviour as well.I found it fascinating that minute data factors like position of the face and distance from the camera can affect the results as well.

I personally found this topic interesting because I do sometimes get bored or lose focus during lectures. But from a professional point of view, I think the idea could be useful for understanding student engagement and improving the way lectures are conducted.However, I also think such a system would need to be designed carefully because facial expressions do not always accurately represent someone's attention or understanding

Overall, this article helped me understand a practical use of Computer Vision and made me curious about how accurately technology can understand human behaviour. It also made me think about the limitations of using facial expressions alone and what other factors could be combined with them to get more reliable results.
